### Collaborative GPT Workflows

In this blog post, we'll introduce a collaborative workflow using our custom GPT agents to create a unique piece of concept art. This step-by-step guide will show how each agent contributes to the final artwork, highlighting the efficiency and creativity of using GPT group chats for collaborative projects.

To follow along with this workflow, you need OpenAI ChatGPT Custom GPT access and to have had at least one conversation with the following Promptfolio GPTs: [Art Collaborator](https://chat.openai.com/g/g-H4LNiC2oY-art-collaborator), [Brainstormer](https://chat.openai.com/g/g-o7HL1rpw9-brainstormer), [Copy Writer](https://chat.openai.com/g/g-aCImhDD6k-copy-writer), and [Concept Artist](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-P75Zn6wVQ-concept-artist).

After you start one conversation with each GPT, they will be available to '@' mention from your other conversations. If you mention a custom GPT from a default ChatGPT 4o conversation, the custom GPTs will use GPT-4o to generate responses informed by the context of that conversation, including custom instructions and memory.

This workflow example is a concise implementation of a collaborative creative process. You can adapt and expand on this model to suit your specific project needs. If you're looking for GPT collaborators on software development, custom GPTs, or visual art projects, [Promptfolio](https://www.promptfolio.dev/listing) is here to help bring your ideas to life.
