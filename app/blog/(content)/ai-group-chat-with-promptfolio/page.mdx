### Collaborative GPT Workflows

In this blog post, we're sharing a simplified collaborative workflow using Promptfolio custom GPTs to create a unique piece of concept art. This step-by-step guide illustrates how each agent contributes to the final artwork, demonstrating the efficiency and ease of indulging your creativity when using Promptfolio GPT group chats for collaborative projects.

To follow along with this workflow, you need OpenAI ChatGPT Custom GPT access and to have had at least one conversation with the following Promptfolio GPTs: [Art Collaborator](https://chat.openai.com/g/g-H4LNiC2oY-art-collaborator), [Brainstormer](https://chat.openai.com/g/g-o7HL1rpw9-brainstormer), [Copy Writer](https://chat.openai.com/g/g-aCImhDD6k-copy-writer), and [Concept Artist](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-P75Zn6wVQ-concept-artist).

After you have met a GPT by starting at least one conversation with them, they will be available to '@' mention from other conversations to create a group chat. If you mention a custom GPT from a default ChatGPT 4o conversation, the custom GPTs will use GPT-4o to generate responses informed by the context of that conversation, including custom instructions and memory.

This workflow example is a concise implementation of a collaborative visual art process. You can adapt and expand on this model to suit your specific project needs. If you're looking for GPT collaborators, especially on software application, custom GPT, or visual art projects, [Promptfolio](https://www.promptfolio.dev/listing) is here to help bring your ideas to life.

### Collaborative Art Creation Process

#### 1. Establish Context with Art Collaborator

To begin, start a conversation with Art Collaborator to establish the context for our collaborative art project. Discuss the theme, style, and specific requirements for the piece with as much or as little detail as you enjoy. Art Collaborator is directed to help clarify your vision, ensuring a solid foundation for the project.
