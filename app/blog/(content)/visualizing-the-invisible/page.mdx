### My Journey with Aphantasia and AI

Living with aphantasia means I navigate the world without the ability to voluntarily visualize mental images. Imagine reading a book without being able to picture the scenes or characters—this is my reality every day. Though I sometimes dream in color, in my waking life, even my most focused efforts cannot summon the simplest mental images. In this context, my encounter with AI, particularly through language models and generative art tools, has opened a new frontier for me. It provides a way to visualize ideas and memories that remain elusive in my mind's eye.

### Rediscovering Art with AI

Back in the early days of Photoshop and digital art, I found joy in using distortion tools, brushes, and layers to create psychedelic tie-dye backgrounds behind silhouettes from iconic Jimi Hendrix photos. This project was a nod to the hippie counterculture-inspired mass-produced tapestries that adorned the room of a '90s kid like myself. More recently, through conversational access to DALL•E via ChatGPT, I felt inspired to pick up where I left off on that project decades ago. Throughout this post, I'll share selections from those sessions, hoping to inspire your next creative pursuit, but I am grateful just to have my artwork appreciated by others.

### Living with Aphantasia

Aphantasia has profoundly shaped how I perceive and interact with the world. My inability to visualize has often limited my enjoyment of traditional visual arts, pushing me instead toward creative writing and coding—domains where language and logic reign. Whether it's crafting narratives or converting complex mockups into CSS, I achieve a state of creative flow through composition rather than visual creation.

### AI as a Creative Catalyst

The advent of generative AI art interfaces like DALL•E has been transformative for me. These tools translate textual descriptions into visual artworks, allowing me to bridge the gap between linguistic creativity and visual expression. The iterative feedback and modification process of these AI tools has fostered a dynamic, collaborative space between the technology and me, alleviating frustration and opening new avenues for creative expression.

### The Broader Impact

For people with aphantasia, AI opens up new experiences with visual creativity previously off-limits. Understanding the world through a natural language filter is no longer just a workaround but a distinct advantage when communicating with AI. This technology can level the playing field, turning what were once perceived limitations into new possibilities for expression and interaction.

This journey with AI hasn't just changed how I engage with art; it has fundamentally reshaped how I view my capabilities, transforming a limitation into a unique tool for personal and artistic expression. This novel technology enables those of us who cannot visualize in the traditional sense to imagine, create, and explore visual artistry in our own unique ways. I am just getting started.
